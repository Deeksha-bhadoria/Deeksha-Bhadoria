## 📌 Featured Project

### [Healthcare Claims Revenue Analytics](https://github.com/Deeksha-bhadoria/Revenue-analytics)
*Python · SQL · Power BI*

End-to-end analysis of 1,000 healthcare insurance claims to identify revenue leakage and denial patterns.

- Identified a **32.8% claim denial rate** vs. the 15% industry average
- Quantified **$96,437 in uncollected revenue** across claim categories
- Pinpointed authorization failures as the top denial driver (14.6% of denials)
- Delivered findings through a 2-page interactive Power BI dashboard

> The kind of project where the numbers have real stakes — every percentage point in denial rate is money a provider didn't get paid.
